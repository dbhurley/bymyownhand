## Why This Matters

T-Mobile's transition is emblematic of a broader trend where companies are increasingly re-evaluating their cloud strategies. Here are a few critical implications:

1. **Increased Vulnerabilities**: As systems shift, they often expose vulnerabilities that can be exploited. Without proper verification mechanisms, organizations risk losing critical data integrity during these transitions.
2. **Compliance Risks**: Legal challenges often come with compliance scrutiny. Companies must ensure that their documentation is verifiable to avoid regulatory pitfalls, especially when migrating sensitive data.
3. **Trust Erosion**: Stakeholders expect transparency in how organizations manage their data. If document authenticity cannot be guaranteed during significant infrastructure changes, trust may erode, impacting stakeholder confidence.
